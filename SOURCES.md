## Data-handling notes

- FY25 refers to the year ended 31 March 2025; FY26 refers to the year ended 31 March 2026.
- SBI's FY26 annual report uses a restated FY25 comparative NII figure of ₹166,340 crore in the report used for this project. Use the same comparative basis when calculating SBI's NII growth.
- Keep standalone and consolidated figures separate; do not mix them in a single comparison.
- If a bank's reported ratio or comparative value differs between its annual report and a presentation, document the source and basis used rather than silently mixing figures.
